🧠 What if customer support actually remembered you?

I just built SupportMind AI — a support platform where every resolved ticket
becomes long-term memory, powered by Hindsight, and every new ticket starts
by checking that memory before doing anything else.

The result: a customer reports a PDF upload crash → the AI investigates,
fixes it, and saves the resolution. Weeks later, the same issue comes back →
the AI opens with "Welcome back — clearing your Chrome cache fixed this last
time. Same setup?" A different customer with no history gets a fresh
investigation instead of a canned answer.

That's the whole point: memory that's visible, customer-specific, and
genuinely earned — not decorative.
